---

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

**Designing for feeling** (Raja Vijayaraman's principle — Izzul's north star).
Design is not decoration. Design communicates through:
- Color temperature and contrast
- Motion and timing (things that breathe feel alive)
- Spatial awareness (whitespace is not empty — it is weight)
- Feedback and microinteraction (the system responds to you)
- Typography as voice (typeface = personality)

**Function is table stakes. Feeling is the moat.**
